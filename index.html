Wallwatch Support

How do I unlock the next stage?

Clear all five waves in your current stage. Wallwatch saves stage unlocks and your local best score on your device.

Game Center sign-in

Check that you’re signed in to Game Center in your iPhone settings. Then open Wallwatch and choose Settings → Connect to Game Center. You can play the campaign offline.

Controls and audio

Open Wallwatch Settings to resize the controls, adjust music and effects, or turn haptics off. On iPhone Duo, unfold the display to play with the wall along the crease.
