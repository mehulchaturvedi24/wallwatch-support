Privacy Policy

Effective November 13, 2026

What stays on your device

Wallwatch saves control and audio preferences, stage unlocks, and your local best score on your device. The game has no developer account, advertising, or developer analytics. Removing the app removes its locally stored game data from that device.

Optional Game Center

If you connect to Game Center, Apple handles sign-in. Wallwatch uses your Game Center player identifier to associate a run with the signed-in player and sends eligible scores and earned achievements to Apple. Game Center may show this activity according to your Apple account settings. You can stop future submissions by signing out of Game Center or leaving it disconnected. Manage your Game Center data through Apple.
